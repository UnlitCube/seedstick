# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level, language or background.
  Many people here are not writing in their first language; read generously.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private
  information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are
  not acceptable.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at
**unlit.cube@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove,
edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
